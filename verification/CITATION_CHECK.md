# Citation check: California Attorney General letter and the 58 county election official letters (drafts)

Checked Nov. 24, 2026 (America/Chicago). Statute and constitution text was taken from the California Legislature's official site (leginfo.legislature.ca.gov; it returns a Cloudflare challenge to curl, so each section was read with a web fetch and the text saved by hand in `sources/statutes/`, one file per section with its URL and retrieval date). EO 14399 text is the Federal Register / GPO PDF text; the U.S. Constitution is the National Archives transcript; 52 U.S.C. § 20701 is the Cornell LII page. Every quotation in the AG letter and in all 58 county letters was then matched word for word against those saved files by `scripts/verify_quotes.py`: **376 of 376 quoted strings verified (32 unique quotations, 59 documents)**. Output: `verification/quote_check.csv`, `verification/quote_check_summary.json`. Normalization was limited to whitespace, straight vs curly quotes, a bracketed first letter ([t]he), and a comma or period inside the closing quotation mark.

**Model:** the AG letter follows the Kansas AG letter (`Kansas_Attorney_General_Letter.pdf` in the Kansas repo): DRAFT banner, address block, bold Re: line, numbered sections (1 who I am, opening with the Guarantee Clause; 2 EO 14399 framing and its limits; 3 what state law provides, with "Where California has no exact parallel"; 4 why this matters, as a hypothesis with 2026 unofficial; 5 specific requests, public guidance rather than a formal opinion; 30-day response; 6 closing), "Respectfully, Morissa Benson", numbered Sources. California law replaces Kansas law throughout.
