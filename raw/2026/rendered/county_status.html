***** Report Definitions

 
R – Report
Election Night Report. Reporting will continue until all precincts in the county have reported their Election Night Reports. More reports are to be expected on Election Night.
SF – Semi-Final Unofficial Report
Semi-Final Unofficial Election Night Report. All precincts in the county have reported their Election Night results. County elections officials will continue counting ballots (vote-by-mail, provisional, etc.) during the 30-day post-election ballot counting canvass period. These results are Semi-Final Unofficial until the Secretary of State certifies results of the state contest on December 11, 2026.
U – Update
The county provided an update since the Semi-Final Unofficial Election Night Report. Results are updated as county elections officials process vote-by-mail, provisional, and other ballots during the 30-day post-election ballot counting canvass period. These results are Semi-Final Unofficial Results until the Secretary of State certifies results of the state contest on December 11, 2026.
