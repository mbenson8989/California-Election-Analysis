Unofficial Election Results

Election results are updated as often as new data is received from county elections offices after the polls close at 8:00 p.m. on Election Day. Ballots continue to be counted after Election Day during the canvass period; county elections officials must report final official results to the Secretary of State by December 4, 2026. The Secretary of State will certify the results on December 11, 2026.
