# California ten highest vote-flip counties (2026 UNOFFICIAL)

**Flip, stated honestly.** No California county's Democratic governor vote fell. The flip is: Democratic *registration* fell (25 counties) while Democratic *governor votes* rose. A registration drop is not a vote drop.

**Rank rule.** Among the 25 counties where the Democratic registration count fell 2022 to 2026 and Democratic governor votes rose, rank by the rise in surplus S, 2022 to 2026. S = Democratic governor votes ÷ ballots cast × 100, minus D (Democratic share of registered voters, 15-day report), in percentage points. (On the wiki's other pages this quantity is called G = S − P.) Residual = 2026 S minus the 2026 California surplus fit (S = 24.8929 × D/100 + 7.6085).
