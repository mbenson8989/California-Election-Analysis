# Guarantee Clause post (DRAFT for X, not posted)

**Post text:**
Did you know? It's NOT your job to prove our elections are free and fair. The Guarantee Clause puts that on the government. EO 14399 (Mar. 31, 2026, 91 FR 17125) cites it, and says the feds have an "unavoidable duty" (Art. II) to maintain public confidence in election outcomes.

**Accuracy notes (internal, not part of the post):**
- Art. IV, Sec. 4 is quoted in full on the graphics. The guarantee runs from the federal government, so the post says "the government."
- EO 14399 (signed March 31, 2026, 91 FR 17125) cites Art. IV, Sec. 4 in its authority clause: "the Federal Government's constitutional obligation to guarantee a republican form of Government to every State in the Union, U.S. Const. Art. IV, Sec. 4."
- The phrase "unavoidable duty" is in EO 14399 Sec. 1 and is tied to Article II, not the Guarantee Clause: "The Federal Government has an unavoidable duty under Article II of the Constitution of the United States to enforce Federal law, which includes preventing violations of Federal criminal law and maintaining public confidence in election outcomes." The post therefore writes "(Art. II)" after it.
